Pharmaceutical Inorganic and Analytical Chemistry

Four syllabus sections in one offline HTML application. Select a section below to open its virtual experiments, calculations, quizzes and practical-record exports.

Educational use: This combined app embeds the four individual practical simulators. They are teaching aids, not replacements for the current Indian Pharmacopoeia, prescribed practical manual, validated method or institutional SOP. Verify calculations, factors, acceptance limits and safety requirements before laboratory use.

How to use: Download and open this combined HTML file in Chrome or Microsoft Edge. It works offline; use the tabs to switch between the four practical sections. Each section retains its own export and quiz controls.
